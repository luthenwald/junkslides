LawSays
1Honest effectsan op reports every ID it adds, retires or revives
2Unique IDsno two cells or slides share an ID
3Invariantsevery deck an op can reach is well formed
4Round tripprinting then parsing gives the same deck
5Undoundo and redo restore deck and layout exactly
6Layout coherencerecords belong to live IDs, the rest are orphans
7Valid placementevery stored box keeps a part of itself on the slide
8Pure errorsan op that fails changes nothing
9Normalisationan edit never smuggles in a break or a marker
10Pastecut then paste brings back the ID and its layout

11/13

command

  • j k move between cells
  • o adds a cell, b a slide
  • Enter edits, t makes notes
  • d y p cut, copy, paste
  • u U undo and redo

edit

  • type, then Esc
  • a line of --- starts a slide
  • Alt-Enter splits the cell

canvas

  • drag to place, corner to resize
  • arrows nudge, with Shift resize
  • v variant, a alignment
  • f back into the flow
  • g G finer or coarser snap
